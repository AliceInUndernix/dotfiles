switch sites

Every site with cookies, history, or a place in your lists. Keys: j/k move, / filter (the page opens in it; Enter or Esc goes to the list), x or Space mark, * mark all shown, u unmark all. These act on the marked sites, or else on the selected one: p keep, f forget, c new face, i look up IP, h clear history, J allow JavaScript, T without Tor. Also: a look up all, s read storage, w wipe unkept, r refresh.
