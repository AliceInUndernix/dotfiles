1Sh = one-time Shift. Fn = hold for the function keys (layer 10). . ! ? add a space and capitalise the next letter, , ; : add a space.
Chords: Spc+Red = Enter, Tab+Bksp = Esc, Bksp+Alt = Delete, Ctrl+Red = Ctrl+Shift,
" . = &, " . ! = $, " ' = `, , ' = #, ? , ' = %, / | = \, + " . ! = "... ".
