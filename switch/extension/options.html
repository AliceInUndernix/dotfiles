Fingerprinting

Profiles: every site gets its own believable profile, with faint canvas and audio noise of its own and, with "Resist fingerprinting" off, its own screen and processor count, tied to its own Tor circuit. One IP, one profile; a site sees nothing that links it to another site. The system is always the real one: workers, fonts and scrollbars would give a made-up one away. New circuits or a new identity give every site a new profile. Uniform: the same answers for everyone, with a blank canvas and no Web Audio.
IceCat's own measures: UTC timezone, rounded window size, coarse timers. Keep this on; the lies below build on it.
Used only with uniform fingerprints. Default keeps IceCat's own. The others lie in the headers and in navigator, but not in workers, which a site can ask to catch the lie.
The Accept-Language header and navigator.languages both say en-US.
No WebGL, no cameras or microphones, no gamepads, no speech voices, and a fixed disk quota. Maps and 3D sites will break.
With profiles, faint noise unique to the profile. Uniform: an empty image, the same for everyone.
With profiles, inaudible noise unique to the profile. Uniform: Web Audio is removed, and some players and games will not work.
Used only with uniform fingerprints; profiles claim a screen of their own when "Resist fingerprinting" is off. Never smaller than the window.
